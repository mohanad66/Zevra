# Privacy Policy

*Effective date: September 11, 2026*

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how Zevra collects, uses, stores, and shares your
personal data when you use the Platform.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account data:** name, email, phone number, avatar, and login activity.
- **Verification data:** KYC documents (ID images, selfies) for identity checks.
- **Financial data:** investment amounts, balances, crypto addresses, withdrawal
  and payout records.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device and browser information, and usage logs.

## 3. How we use data
We use your data to create and manage your account and balances; process
investments, withdrawals, and payouts; prevent fraud, money laundering, and
abuse; meet AML/KYC legal duties; provide support; improve and secure the
Platform.

## 4. Legal basis
We process data based on your consent, the performance of our contract with you,
our legitimate interest in a secure service, and legal obligations applicable
to financial and virtual-asset businesses.

## 5. Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with service providers who
help operate the Platform (subject to confidentiality), regulators and law
enforcement where required by law, and professional advisors.

## 6. KYC documents
Verification documents are stored securely, access is restricted to trained
compliance staff, and they are retained only as long as required by law.

## 7. Security
We use encryption in transit, hashed/salted passwords, access controls, and
continuous monitoring. No method of transmission over the internet is 100%
secure.

## 8. Cookies and storage
We use authentication tokens stored on your device to keep you signed in. We do
not use third-party advertising cookies.

## 9. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct,
delete, or export your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

## 10. Data retention
We retain financial and compliance records for as long as required by law
(generally at least five years for AML purposes), and other data while your
account is active.

## 11. International transfers
Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. When data is
transferred across borders we apply appropriate safeguards required by law.

## 12. Children
The Platform is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect
data from minors.

## 13. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes are notified and the effective date
above is updated.

## 14. Contact
For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support through the
Platform.
